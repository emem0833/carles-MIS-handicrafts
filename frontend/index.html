About Carles, Iloilo

Carles was founded on July 1, 1862 and named after Spanish Governor Jose Maria Carles y Dominguez. The first settlers arrived around 1846, led by Francisco Ariola from Jolo, who cleared the area at Punta Bulakawe. As more settlers arrived from Antique and Aklan, the settlement was originally called "Badiang" due to the abundance of wild giant gabi plants in the area.

In 1860, town leaders petitioned Governor Carles to become a municipality. On July 1, 1862, Badiang was officially renamed Carles in his honor. Though demoted to a barrio of Balasan in 1904, the Carles Separation Movement led by Casimiro Andrada successfully regained municipal status on January 1, 1920.

Islas de Gigantes, Carles

Today, Carles is known for its rich fishing grounds and popular tourist destinations like Islas de Gigantes, Sicogon Island, and Cabugao Gamay Island. The municipality relies on fishing, agriculture, and tourism, with local craftsmen creating traditional handicrafts from buri, bamboo, pandan leaves, and shells.
